# Rocket collision mechanics

The rocket carrier and the penetrator have separate durability. Ordinary CBC projectiles
are unchanged. Rocket hull HP is 10/20/40 for small/medium/big, with no AP multiplier.

## Damage and fuzes

- Incoming damage spends hull HP. At zero HP an explosive rocket detonates once and is
  removed. AP loses its engine and guidance, but retains its CBC mass and momentum.
- A normal solid-block or entity impact permanently disables the engine and guidance.
  CBC still decides penetration, ricochet, shatter and removal; hull HP never overrides
  that result. The original impact fuze callback still runs on explosive rockets.
- Timed/other ticking fuzes on explosive rockets retain their original detonation logic.
  A ticking fuze on AP disables the engine instead of deleting the penetrator.
- An impact fuze retains its CBC detonation chance and durability; this change does not
  turn it into a guaranteed-contact fuze.
- Engine failure is synchronized to clients and saved. Loading older rockets removes the
  AP hull bonus while preserving the fraction of hull HP remaining.

## Crossable blocks

At a speed of at least 0.25 blocks/tick, the following collision blocks are cleared without
drops (water in waterlogged blocks is retained). Each crossed block applies its own cost.
These crossings do not spend CBC penetrator mass, trigger an impact fuze or disable an
otherwise intact engine. If a crossing exhausts hull HP, normal hull-failure rules apply.

| Tag in the cbcatfix namespace | Defaults | Hull HP cost | Speed loss |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| rocket_permeable_foliage | Minecraft leaves | 0 | 2% |
| rocket_permeable_fragile | Glass blocks and panes, including stained/tinted glass | 1 | 8% |
| rocket_permeable_soft | Wool, wool carpets, hay | 2 | 12% |

The tags can be overridden/extended by a datapack. Block entities and unbreakable blocks
are excluded. Terrain damage restrictions are respected. At lower speed the normal CBC
collision applies. Grass/flowers with no collision shape are already ignored by CBC's
collision ray; they do not receive an artificial collision cost.

Speed losses multiply: two leaf blocks retain 0.98² = 96.04% of speed before subsequent
flight integration. An intact engine can recover speed in flight. CBC uses a fixed sweep
for the current tick: slowing changes subsequent impact calculations and movement, but
does not retrace a shorter collision ray within that same tick.

Wood, logs, stone, metal, armor, ice and all other untagged collision blocks use CBC's
armor properties. Their penetrator-mass cost is:

    (max(0, armor hardness - projectile penetration) + 1)
        * armor toughness / normal impact speed

There is no second hardness-based hull-HP deduction for solid armor. CBC's mass loss and
shatter are authoritative, and the carrier engine is disabled on impact.

## AP ballistics and flight

AP warhead properties are independently configurable under rockets.<size>.apWarhead:

| Size | durabilityMass | penetration | toughness | deflection |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Small | 2 | 2 | 1 | 0.70 |
| Medium | 3.25 | 2.75 | 1.33 | 0.66 |
| Big | 8 | 2 | 1 | 0.70 |

The big AP default corresponds to the AP Shot used in its recipe. This does not alter
the heavy-autocannon round. Double payload still multiplies penetrator mass by 1.5.
Lightweight construction affects flight inertia, not the mass of its unchanged warhead.
Flight inertia is saved separately and is not reduced by armor penetration. Fuel mass
depletion is not simulated.

Armor checks use CBC/CBCAT's native velocity and force calculation. Calling getForces
calculates a vector without accumulating acceleration in entity state. Light obstacles
reduce velocity; the current native sweep still uses its initially calculated segment.
The remaining CBC mass is never regenerated by the engine. Powered rockets retain
CBC's native orientation after the sweep so velocity alignment does not erase steering.

## Verification

Run the opt-in integration suite in an isolated world:

    gradlew.bat runRocketTests -ProcketTests --offline --console=plain

The tests execute real mixin-transformed projectiles. Test classes and the generated
empty test structure are excluded from the distributable JAR.
